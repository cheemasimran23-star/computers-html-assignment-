Interesting Facts About Computers

Learn a few facts about the machines we use every day.

What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that processes information and helps people complete tasks such as writing, learning, communicating and creating.

Three common types of computers

  • Desktop computers are often used at home and in offices.
  • Laptops are portable computers that can be carried around.
  • Tablets are lightweight devices controlled mainly by a touchscreen.

Computers in everyday life

Computers help people study, work, shop, design products and stay in touch with others.
